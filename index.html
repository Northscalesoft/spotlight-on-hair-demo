About the salon

A friendly chair, a great cut.

Spotlight On Hair is a hair salon on Roberts Avenue in Fremont. We keep things simple: listen first, cut and colour with care, and send you out the door feeling like yourself — only better.

Whether it is a regular trim, a fresh colour, or a style for a special day, call us and we will find a time that works for you.
